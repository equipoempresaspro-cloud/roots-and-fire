Our story

Two chefs, one wok, and a lot of roots.

Chefs Cesar Alonso Vargas and Lady Diana Fajardo came up in The Plaza Hotel, Metropolis by Marcus Samuelsson and Five Acres at Rockefeller Center. Now they cook the Peruvian and Latin flavors they grew up with, over live fire, every weekend in Bushwick.
