Our story

Two chefs, one wok, and a lot of roots.

Roots & Fire was born as a catering company, built by Chefs Cesar Alonso Vargas and Lady Diana Fajardo after years in some of New York's most demanding kitchens — The Plaza Hotel, Metropolis by Marcus Samuelsson and Five Acres at Rockefeller Center.

Roots is where we come from: the Peruvian and Latin flavors we grew up with. Fire is how we cook them: live flame, a screaming-hot wok and restaurant-level technique.

Today we cook for Brooklyn every weekend from our kitchen on Starr Street — and we still bring the full experience to weddings, parties and private events across NYC.

“Every dish we create tells a story.”

— Cesar Alonso, co-founder

Catering & events

Bring the fire to your party.

From a birthday at home to a 200-guest wedding, we cook the same food — scaled up, beautifully presented and served by our team. Peru, Venezuela and New York on one table.

Cocktail reception

Passed bites while your guests mingle: mini arepas, “Lox & Leche de Tigre” bagel bites, Chop Cheese Criollo, Philly Steak Saltado and tequeños.

Party trays

Big trays of lomo saltado, chaufa, wings, tequeños and churros — ready to serve for birthdays, office lunches, game days and family gatherings.

Live-fire stations

Carne en vara cooked in front of your guests — picanha, churrasco, anticucho chicken — plus ceviche bar and criollo wok stations.

Weddings, corporate & private dinners

Full-service menus designed with you, from tasting to the last plate. Chef-at-home dinners for intimate celebrations.
